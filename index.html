ABAHA Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

This policy explains how the ABAHA iOS game handles information when you play. The developer does not directly collect personal data or operate a server that receives your personal information. ABAHA does use Apple Game Center for online gameplay and rankings, and the Unity Ads SDK for optional rewarded video ads. These services may process information under their own privacy policies.

Information stored on your device

ABAHA stores game progress, settings, and saved games locally on your device so you can continue playing. The developer does not receive this locally stored information. Removing the app may remove its local data, subject to your device's backup and restore settings.

Apple Game Center

ABAHA uses Apple Game Center for turn-based online matches, leaderboards, achievements, and the Game Center sign-in experience. When you use these features, Apple may process your Game Center identity, player name, scores, achievements, match information, and related gameplay activity. ABAHA accesses the Game Center information needed to display and operate these features, but does not create a separate ABAHA account or store this information on a developer-operated server. You can use ABAHA's offline modes without a Game Center sign-in. Apple's handling of Game Center information is described in its Game Center & Privacy notice.

Optional rewarded video ads

ABAHA includes the Unity Ads SDK to load and display optional rewarded video ads. Unity may collect or process information such as device identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from IP address, and ad interactions to deliver ads, measure performance, prevent fraud, and perform other purposes described in Unity's Privacy Policy. The ad SDK may communicate with Unity when the app starts or loads an ad, even if you choose not to watch one. The developer does not directly receive personal data collected by Unity through the SDK.

When applicable, iOS asks for App Tracking Transparency permission before the first rewarded ad is shown. You can change tracking permission in iOS Settings. Declining permission does not prevent you from using ABAHA's offline gameplay; it may affect ad personalization. You choose whether to watch a rewarded ad.

Your choices and data requests

You can play offline without Game Center. You can manage Game Center participation and privacy settings through your Apple device settings and Apple account. You can manage app tracking permission through Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Unity's privacy policy explains its available privacy controls and how to make requests about information it processes. To remove ABAHA's local game data, delete the app from your device; restored backups may retain a copy.

Children

ABAHA does not ask players to submit personal information directly to the developer. Apple and Unity may apply their own age-related settings and protections. Parents and guardians can use Apple's parental controls and Game Center restrictions to manage a child's access to online features and ads.

Changes to this policy

If ABAHA's data practices change, this page will be updated and the effective date above will be revised.

Contact

For questions about this policy or ABAHA's data practices, contact the developer at mitkonaft@gmail.com.
